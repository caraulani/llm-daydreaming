# Datasheet: <slice name>

Follows Gebru et al., "Datasheets for Datasets" (2021). Answer every question; write "N/A" with a reason where it does not apply.

## Motivation

- For what purpose was the dataset created?
- Who created it, and on behalf of which entity?
- Who funded it?

## Composition

- What do the instances represent (documents, concept cards, dreams, verdicts)?
- How many instances are there?
- Is it a sample of a larger set? If so, how was the sample drawn, and is it representative?
- What does each instance consist of? List the fields.
- Is there a label or target? Which field?
- Is any information missing? Why?
- Are relationships between instances made explicit (source pointers, lineage)?
- Recommended splits, if any (for example, a temporal holdout).
- Known errors, noise, or redundancies.
- Does it rely on external resources (arXiv, OpenAlex)? Are they stable? Archived?
- Does it contain confidential data? Personal data? Data that could identify a person?
- Does it contain anything offensive or sensitive?

## Collection process

- How was the data acquired (direct observation, API, generated by a model)?
- Which mechanisms or software? Were they validated?
- Sampling strategy, if sampled.
- Who was involved in collection, and how were they compensated?
- Over what time frame was it collected? Does that match the creation time of the underlying content?
- For generated data: model snapshot IDs, prompt SHAs, sampling parameters, access dates.
- Ethical review (IRB or equivalent)? If none, say so.
- For personal data: were the individuals notified, did they consent, can they revoke?

## Preprocessing

- Was any preprocessing, cleaning, or labelling done (secret stripping, deduplication, chunking)? Describe it.
- Is the raw data available in addition to the processed data?
- Is the preprocessing software available? Where?

## Uses

- What has it been used for so far? Link papers or runs.
- What else could it be used for?
- Is there anything about its composition or collection that could affect future uses (contamination with model training data, for example)?
- Are there uses it should not be put to?

## Distribution

- Will it be distributed to third parties? How (GitHub, Hugging Face, DOI)?
- When?
- Under what license? (`CC-BY-4.0` unless the source forbids it.)
- Any third-party restrictions (source terms of use)?
- Export controls or regulatory restrictions?

## Maintenance

- Who supports and hosts it? Contact.
- Is there an erratum?
- Will it be updated? How will consumers be told?
- For personal data: retention limits?
- Will older versions remain available?
- How can others contribute or extend it?
