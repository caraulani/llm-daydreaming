# Packaging Insert Redesign: QR Linking

## Current state
- Old insert was 1.5 pages, dense instruction blocks. About 15% of customer queries were about wash/dry procedures.
- Redesigned to half-page format: materials, washing, storage as separate sections with visual hierarchy.
- QR code added, links to product page care tab hosted on Shopify, static CDN delivery.

## QR testing notes
- Printed on kraft insert stock, 280 gsm matte finish. QR size 2cm x 2cm, readable at arm's length.
- Link tested on older Android phones and iPhone 12. Both resolve in under 2 seconds over mobile.
- Mobile page loads care instructions and videos sourced from supplier. Built with Liquid templating, no external dependencies.

## Next steps
- Pilot batch of 500 units printing next week. Track redirect hits in Shopify analytics, target 10% scan rate within 30 days.
- Monitor support ticket volume before and after rollout to measure impact.
- Test video embeds on slow connections (3G fallback), check page render time.

## Known issues
- QR prints poorly on thermal label printers. Use offset printing only. Supplier confirmed next batch switches to gravure.
- Mobile layout breaks below 320px width, rare but needs flagging.
