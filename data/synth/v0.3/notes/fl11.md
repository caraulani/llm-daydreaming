## Chargeback Representment Template

## Evidence Collection
- Capture timestamp of fraudulent claim: exact transaction time, user IP, device fingerprint
- Payment processor gives 10 days to submit; first 3 days critical for case clarity
- Pull call logs: duration, number dialed, caller account status at charge time
- Screenshots of user consent flow (ToS acceptance, payment method confirmation)
- Email confirmations sent to account holder if automated receipt was delivered

## Template Structure
- Header: transaction ID, dispute date, amount, merchant reference
- Narrative: what service was rendered, when, how user initiated the call
- Evidence list: ordered by relevance (call logs first, then consent screens, timestamps)
- Rebuttal section: address specific cardholder claim type (unauthorized transaction, service not rendered, duplicate charge)
- Footer: case number reference, response date, legal entity signature

## Submission Notes
- Some processors require PDF; others accept scanned images. Check your processor's portal
- Call logs must show direction (inbound/outbound), duration, and CLID data if available
- Keep one master template; vary only the narrative and evidence per case
- Response deadline is processor-specific: Visa 21 days, Mastercard 45 days after initial filing
