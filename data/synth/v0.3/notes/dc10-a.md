Switched carriers for fragile shipments after two losses in one month

## Problem
- Lost package on 2023-04-15 : vase from Italy, $98.76 insurance claim filed.
- Another loss on 2023-05-01 : custom glassware, $42.12 refund issued.

## Solution
- Shipped fragile items via UPS starting 2023-05-15.
- Set up tracking alerts for all shipments.
- Reviewed insurance policies with new carrier.

## Monitoring
- No further losses since switch on 2023-05-15.
- Continue to monitor other packages sent via original carrier.
- Will review carrier reliability data in one month.
