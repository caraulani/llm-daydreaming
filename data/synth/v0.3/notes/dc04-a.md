New Library Rolled Out
----------------------
- New device-fingerprint library deployed at 08:00 AM UTC on 19 August.
- Initial testing phase over two weeks showed mixed results.

False Positives Increased
-------------------------
- False positive rate rose by 3% from previous week's baseline of 2.5%.
- Most false positives occurred during peak usage hours: 14:00 to 18:00 UTC.

Next Steps Planned
------------------
- Review logs for patterns in false positives over next few days.
- Consider adjusting thresholds if necessary to stabilize detection rates.
