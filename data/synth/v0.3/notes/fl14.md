New Pharmacy Portal Launched

## Features Added
- Online refill requests simplified
- Prescription history viewable, sortable by date
- Secure payment option for prescriptions added

## Pickup Timing Adjusted
- New 2-hour pick-up window introduced
- Notifications sent at appointment time and half hour before pickup slot starts
- Option to extend pick-up window by contacting pharmacy staff

## Personal Protocol Update Required
- Review refill schedule with new portal features
- Confirm medication orders within 24 hours of notification for pickup optimization
- Adjust travel plans around updated 2-hour pick up times
