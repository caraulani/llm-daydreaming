## SPRING24 Redemption Cluster

May 3rd brought 41 redemptions of SPRING24 between 02:05 and 02:45. Processing was clean, no issues. The concentration in that 40-minute window is worth tracking as the month continues.

## Shipping and Item Selection

Fulfillment review on the May 3rd orders shows a uniform pattern: every customer selected cheapest shipping. Same story on items, each picked the lowest-priced option in their browsed category. Makes operational sense for discount seekers, but the uniformity is odd enough to flag for future promo runs.

## Customer Records

Email addresses for this group all follow postbin.io format with four-digit prefixes. Range: 0410 through 0451, completely sequential with no gaps. Recording this in CRM for reference.

## Inventory Impact

41 units sold across multiple categories, mostly lower-tier stock moved. Will adjust product mix if the pattern repeats in other promotions.
