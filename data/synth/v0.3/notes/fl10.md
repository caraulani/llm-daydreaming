Velocity Limits Tweak

## Goals
- Reduce fraudulent activity on payment platform.
- Maintain smooth user experience for legitimate transactions.

## Metrics
- Initial spike detected at 5 top-ups/device/day.
- Fraudulent attempts peaked around 7 top-ups/device/day.

## Adjustments Made
- Set velocity limit to 4 top-ups/device/day.
- Implemented a gradual increase: +1 every 30 days if no fraud detected.
- Monitored for side-effects on user satisfaction.

## Results Observed
- Decrease in reported fraudulent activities by 25% within first month.
- Average daily transactions per device stabilized below adjusted limit.
