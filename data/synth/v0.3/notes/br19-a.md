Bedroom CO2 readings, early data

Installed the CO2 sensor on 20 August. Early pattern: overnight readings consistently 1,800 to 1,950 ppm between 02:00 and 06:00. Living room sensor maxes out around 900 ppm in the same window. Cable routed through the window frame, sealed with foam on installation day.

## Sensor placement notes

The two-unit setup gives useful contrast. Living room stays below 900 ppm throughout the day. Bedroom shows the expected accumulation overnight, peaks in the 1,900 ppm range reliably. Both sensors logging to the local instance.

## Cable management

Fed the sensor line through the existing frame after foam sealing. Slight cable tension at the frame edge, but stable. No data dropout so far. Considered conduit but the direct route works.

## Next steps

Let the sensors run for another week of baseline data before adjusting anything. Logging looks clean. The temperature compensation on both units seems reasonable, no drift visible yet.
