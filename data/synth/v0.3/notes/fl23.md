# Cable drawer labelling system

## Current state
- Drawer 3 overflowing. 50+ cables mixed together
- Can't find micro USB to USB-C adapters when needed
- Lost two RS485 terminators last month
- Existing labels faded or missing

## Colour scheme
- Red: power (mains, barrel connectors)
- Blue: data (USB, serial, network)
- Yellow: sensor connections (JST, 3.5mm, specialty connectors)
- Green: IoT device specific (LoRaWAN antenna, Zigbee dongle, etc.)
- White: obsolete or untested

## Action items
- Print durable labels with label maker (waterproof adhesive)
- Coil each cable, secure with matching velcro straps
- Inventory spreadsheet: cable type, gauge, length, location
- Move drawer 3 contents to cabinet 1 for better access
- Time estimate: 4 hours total

## Outcome
Reduced search time from 10+ minutes to under 2 minutes for standard cables.
