# IRR baseline for the two labellers

## Setup
Two annotators scored 150 sampled outputs from the EvalKit corpus. Cohen's kappa 0.72 on the main rubric (binary keep/kill on coherence). Disagreements clustered on borderline examples: n=18 cases where one rater saw acceptable paraphrase, the other saw semantic drift.

## Per-dimension breakdown
- Overall correctness: k=0.78
- Fluency: k=0.85
- Factual grounding: k=0.64
- Completeness vs brevity: k=0.59 (weakest; raters had different length priors)

## Adjudication
Sent the 18 disagreements back with a tie-break rule: if either rater marked uncertain, default to kill. Flipped 4 decisions (mostly haiku outputs marked uncertain by the stricter rater). Final agreement on the disputed set: 96%.

## Action items
- Tighten the rubric on what counts as acceptable brevity, completeness tradeoff
- Fresh sample with refined guidance before locking this as the baseline
- Check whether kappa improves with more concrete examples in the annotation sheet
