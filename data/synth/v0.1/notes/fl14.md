# Morning Walk Protocol

## Current routine

Walk 6am before the store opens, around the neighborhood. 45 minutes most days. Aim for 8000 steps minimum, 10000 ideal. Phone tracks via built-in step counter. Post-walk coffee, then email check before 7am opening prep.

## Step targets by season

Winter (Nov-Feb): 8000 steps, adjusted for weather. Summer (Jun-Aug): 10000 steps, faster pace. Spring/fall: 9000 steps. Weekends slightly flexible but maintain minimum 7500.

## Tracking method

iPhone Health app syncs automatically. Log weekly totals in a spreadsheet. Current average: 9200 steps. Miss target 1-2 days per month (weather or late store closings).

## Integration notes

Walk time doubles as thinking space for store decisions and client evaluations. Skip walk means worse focus on LLM assessment work. Walking before notifications hit helps concentration. Temperature below 5C or rain above 15mm typically triggers shorter route (35 min, still around 7500 steps).
