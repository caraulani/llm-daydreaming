# Outdoor Humidity Sensor Enclosure

## Candidates Evaluated

- Polycarbonate IP67 box (78×56×35mm): clear face, UV stable, $12. Passthrough for probe.
- Aluminum NEMA 4X (100×80×40mm): weatherproof, $28, overkill for single sensor.
- 3D printed ABS custom: $3 filament cost, need UV coating or degrades by month 3.
- PVC junction box with rubber gasket: $4, proven 2 years at shed, yellows over time.

## Selection: Clear Polycarbonate Plus External Probe

Going polycarbonate. Sensor stays outside enclosure, probe threaded through bottom gland. Accurate humidity readings, no water pooling inside. Can see circuit board for diagnostics without opening.

## Mounting

South wall, 2m height, slight downward tilt. Drip loop on power cable. First inspection at 90 days after rain. Undecided on fastener: adhesive or steel plate clamp. Power runs conduit 4m to breaker.

## Open

UV treatment needed. Bare polycarbonate turns brown in 18 months. Testing two options: spray-on polycarbonate coating or adhesive acrylic overlay. Spray is simpler; overlay adds bulk.
