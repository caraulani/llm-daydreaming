## Timeline

Magnesium glycinate 500mg nightly starting Sept 5. Oura ring began same week. Sleep improved noticeably within 2-3 days: middle-night waking decreased, longer continuous rest, subjective quality +2-3 points.

## Ring reads, week 1

Captures HRV, resting HR, deep sleep %, REM %, sleep latency, movement. First 7 days: 7.2 avg hours, 75% deep, 65 bpm resting. No weeks with ring-only baseline before supplement. Both changes in the same observational window.

## Method

Log each morning: bedtime, wake, quality 1-10, notes. Ring syncs daily to phone. Plan to continue both for 2-3 weeks, watch for stability.
