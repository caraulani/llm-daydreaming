# Flagship product pricing benchmark

## Current state

Selling at $89 USD. Competitor A (larger marketplace) at $76, Competitor B (direct seller) at $94. Margin pressure from both sides.

## Competitor A analysis

$76 seems to be volume play. They bulk-source components at ~$18 per unit (estimate from shipping weights). Running heavy ad spend, likely 15-20% margin. Turnover looks fast, inventory moves weekly. Elasticity probably high; they're price-anchor.

## Competitor B analysis

$94 hits the premium segment. Smaller operation, similar to mine. Focus on customization options (adds perceived value). Shipping to EU included. Probably 25-30% margin. Slower turnover but better per-unit math.

## Options

- Hold at $89. Undercut Competitor A just enough to matter ($71-74 range). Requires cost reduction or accept lower margin.
- Move to $92-95 like Competitor B. Adds customization tiers, repositions away from race-to-bottom. More work upfront.
- Seasonal pricing. Keep base at $89, run $79 in Q4 when they do, protect margin in slow months.
