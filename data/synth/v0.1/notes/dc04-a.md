## Morning Stack Protocol

Current rotation started Sept 1. Ordering revised after magnesium switch.

## Sequence and Timing

- 06:30: Water + electrolyte mix (sodium, potassium, trace minerals)
- 06:35: Omega-3 (2g, Nordic Naturals, batch 2026-06), vitamin D3 (4000 IU)
- 06:40: Magnesium glycinate (400mg, switched from citrate in August; less GI irritation)
- 06:50: Breakfast with zinc + copper (15mg/2mg ratio), B-complex
- Afternoon (14:00): Vitamin C (500mg, ascorbic acid), iron supplement (ferrous bisglycinate, 20mg on Mon/Wed/Fri only)

## Recent Changes

- Magnesium citrate discontinued Aug 28, switched to glycinate. Digestion improved.
- Removed separate calcium supplement (getting enough from diet). Zinc timing moved to breakfast.
- Monitoring energy levels week-to-week. Track in spreadsheet row.
