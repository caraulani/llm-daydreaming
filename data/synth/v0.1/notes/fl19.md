# Horizon Europe SME Instrument vs Regional Dev Fund: key differences

## Instrument scope and fit

**Horizon Europe SME (Phase 2):**
- 500K to 2.5M EUR ceiling
- Emphasis on innovation, R&D intensity expected
- Focus on disruptive tech, scalable solutions
- International consortium often preferred but solo applicants eligible in some calls
- Very competitive, long review cycle (4-6 months)

**Regional Development Fund (our region):**
- 100K to 800K EUR typical
- Digitalization, process improvement, local market fit acceptable
- Solo entrepreneurs and micro-enterprises prioritized
- Faster turnaround (6-8 weeks)
- Co-financing requirement varies by measure

## Application load and timeline

Horizon Europe: 40-50 page proposal, technical appendices, Gantt charts, cost breakdown, letter from financial auditor required.

Regional: 15-20 page application, simpler financial forms, existing business plan suffices.

## Deliverables and reporting

Horizon Europe: monthly reports on milestones, EU portal submissions, possible audit visits, 2-year commitment minimum.

Regional: quarterly expense reports, once-annual impact statement, 18-month project period.

## Strategic fit for current situation

Horizon smarter if scaling e-commerce stack or developing new service offering. Regional better for immediate cash flow and lower administrative overhead.
