# Client wants gate over gradient

## The ask

Client says their internal team needs "pass" or "fail", not a continuous score. They're evaluating three Claude variants for a support automation task. Requested decision boundary at 0.7 on my scoring rubric. They want to bin outputs: above threshold goes to production, below goes to human review queue.

## Current setup

Running comparative evals now: Haiku vs Sonnet vs Opus on 200 support tickets. Each output gets scored 0 to 1 on coherence, factuality, tone match. Right now they see the full distribution. They want a cutoff.

## Implications

- Can't report nuance below/above line. Loses the 0.68 vs 0.72 distinction they might need later.
- Have to commit to the threshold now, audit it post-hoc if volume shows skew.
- Simpler for their ops team though. Less variance in their review process.

## Next

- Propose 0.65 threshold instead (more conservative, catches borderline cases).
- Test on 50 tickets first, measure false positives in the human queue.
- Lock threshold in contract once they sign off.
