# Eligibility gatekeeping in Horizon

## The screening wall

Preliminary eligibility is strict: company age, technology maturity level, sector fit, prior work track record, funder alignment. About forty percent of applications don't clear these gates. The criteria exist but no single document lists all five points. Took two phone calls to the helpline to get the full picture.

## Timing cost

Spent three weeks writing the full application narrative, preparing demos, sourcing letters. Only found out I'd cleared the screening stage after I'd submitted everything. Talked to other applicants: same pattern. They spend weeks on drafts, then get the rejection letter. The writing happens before the gate check.

## Next round preparation

Need to reverse the order. Run through each eligibility gate manually before writing a single word. If the answer is no on any of the five points, the application work is wasted.
