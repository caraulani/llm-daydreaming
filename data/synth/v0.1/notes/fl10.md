# Incident Timeline: Payment Processor Fraud

## Event Summary
Detected unusual transaction pattern 2026-09-10 at 14:32 UTC. Multiple failed auth attempts (47 in 12 minutes) followed by 3 successful charges totaling $1,847. Processor flagged at 15:05, account frozen.

## Timeline
- 2026-09-10 14:32 UTC: First failed charge (visa ending 4521)
- 2026-09-10 14:44 UTC: 46 additional failures, 3 successes on different cards
- 2026-09-10 15:05 UTC: Processor automated hold, emailed alerts
- 2026-09-10 16:20 UTC: Confirmed fraud, initiated chargeback process
- 2026-09-11 08:00 UTC: Retrieved transaction logs, matched IP origin
- 2026-09-12 10:30 UTC: Submitted incident report to processor with supporting docs

## Cards Affected
Visa 4521, Mastercard 5105, Amex 3782. Customer accounts contacted. Two chargebacks filed successfully. One pending.

## Actions Taken
Rate limiting increased to 3 attempts per 10 minutes. Notified customers of breach window. Payment processor upgraded to 3DS verification on all transactions pending.
