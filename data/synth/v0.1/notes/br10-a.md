Cycle Churn and Engagement Gaps

## Third Cycle Cancellations
Cancellations spike at the third billing period. Cohort data confirms this. Pattern holds across product tiers. Need to identify what triggers it and whether it's preventable with earlier intervention.

## Skipped Delivery Fallout
Customers who skip one scheduled delivery almost never return. Skip in cycle 2 maps to churn. Unclear whether it's disengagement or friction on restart, but the correlation is solid. Testing re-engagement after skips.

## Silent Periods
Nothing ships between regular deliveries. Customers go invisible for weeks. No touchpoint, no visibility into their state. This gap might matter. Considering low-contact check-ins during quiet stretches.

## Next Steps
- Full cohort breakdown by cycle number
- Separate skippers from other cancellers
- Test outreach timing immediately after skip events, before the next cycle window
