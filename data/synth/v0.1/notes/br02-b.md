Resubmission workflow update

## Funder changes for this round

The call moved to fully anonymized review panels starting 2025. Means no reviewer carries forward prior knowledge. Shifts how to frame the resubmission, different angle needed even if core idea same.

## Resubmission strategy

Found it helps to reference the numerical feedback from last round when it's constructive. Not framing, just data points. Seems to land better with fresh reviewers who haven't anchored on the previous panel's take. Counterintuitive.

## Self-scoring glitch

Internal rubric tool always pulls up last year's scores first when I open it to draft new sections. Annoying UI pattern. Have to scroll past them to see fresh criteria. Made a note to start with blank template instead.

## Next deadline

Consolidated three strand materials by end August. Trimmed budget narrative. Targeting submission by early October for the broader innovation window. Panel probably convenes December.
