# Budget template: Personnel costs framework

## Cost ceilings and hourly rates

Personnel costs capped at 215 EUR/hour (standard 2024 rates). Solo applicants can claim at simplified unit costs if turnover under threshold: 1850 EUR/month typical baseline. Differentiate between management time (higher rate) and delivery time (lower). Need to log actual hours; estimates don't survive audit.

## What counts as personnel vs subcontracting

My time coding/testing on the grant = personnel. Contractor QA specialist I bring in = subcontracting budget line. Blurry zone: freelance consultants I pay hourly (can go either way if under 30% of total, safer as personnel co-rate). Family members: prohibited, avoid entirely.

## Documentation required

Timesheets per person per month, even if simplified rates. Cost breakdown by activity (development, testing, management). Indirect costs (workshop space, utilities) either flat-rate overhead (~25% if eligible) or itemised. Keep invoices from anyone I pay, even internal allocations.

## Thresholds and gotchas

Under 40k EUR budget: simplified unit costs available, fewer audit triggers. Over 40k: full cost substantiation. Mixed income (store revenue + grant funds) needs clear separation; can't double-dip on equipment depreciation. Salary increases mid-project: retroactive updates require justification, easier to set fixed monthly rate upfront.
