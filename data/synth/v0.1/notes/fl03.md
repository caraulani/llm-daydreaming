# Returns Policy and Common Complaints

## Current Policy Wording

Wording revision needed: "30 days from order date" creates confusion because tracking delivery varies by region. Customers interpret this as 30 days from receiving, not shipping. Changed to "30 days from delivery" but tracking API shows 15% missing final mile confirmation for international orders.

## Most Common Complaint: Missing Return Shipping Label

Customers expect pre-printed labels in packages. Implemented this Q2 but clarity gap remains. Complaint volume hasn't dropped because policy page shows no mention of labels before purchase. Revision: front-load this on product pages, not just FAQ.

## Second Most Common: Refund Timing

Stated: 5-7 business days after item received and inspected. Reality: inspection takes 2-3 days, processing another 2. Customers reach out after 5 days seeing no credit. Change to "up to 14 days" or add refund status tracking portal. Testing email notifications at inspection milestone.

## Action Items

- Update product page copy to highlight included return labels
- Add refund status tracking link to returns confirmation email
- Test "refund in progress" notifications

This covers maybe 60% of returns inquiries. Other complaints are condition disputes or weight differences on international shipments.
