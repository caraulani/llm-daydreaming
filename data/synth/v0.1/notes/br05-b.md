Two applications, same deadline (July 15, HORIZON SME track). Content review after submission showed overlap in case study material and methodology descriptions. Both companies registered in 2024, a few months apart.

## Funder procedures

Grants office mentioned they cross-check applications against registries during initial screening. They look for institutional connections and linked entities as standard verification. Evaluation phase hasn't started yet. No feedback so far.
