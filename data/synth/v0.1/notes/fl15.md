## Hydration and Caffeine Cutoff

## Daily hydration targets

- 3 liters minimum on desk days (audit, email, calls)
- 4 liters on build days (workshop, customer calls, testing)
- Track via water bottle marks: 8am, 12pm, 4pm, 8pm
- Morning: 500ml with electrolyte salt (sodium 300mg) on non-food days
- Afternoon slump at 2pm: 750ml + 10 min walk usually restores focus

## Caffeine rules

- Last espresso by 12:30pm, no exceptions
- Cold brew cutoff 1pm on sleep-critical nights (EU grant deadlines, next-day client evals)
- One 200mg pill acceptable if before 11am
- Afternoon energy: herbal tea only after 3pm, or nothing
- Tested: 1pm cutoff gives decent sleep; 2pm cutoff causes 30 min sleep onset delay

## Sleep impact log

- Caffeine after 1pm on audit nights: 40 min longer to fall asleep
- Hydration deficit (under 2.5L) plus late coffee: groggy next morning, eval accuracy drops
- Proper hydration + 12:30pm cutoff: sleep by 11:15pm, wake 6am sharp

## Storage and prep

- Thermos filled 6:30am, refills at desk
- Electrolyte packets in audit bag and workshop
- Espresso maker runs 7am, noon slots blocked on calendar
