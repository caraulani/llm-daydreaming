## Two-Factor Prompts and Signup Completion

Testing when to ask for 2FA in the calling app signup. Early ask blocks ~12% of users; late ask (after first payment) drops that to 3-4%.

## Flow Variants Tested

- **Variant A (immediate)**: 2FA at signup, before email verify. Completion: 73%.
- **Variant B (after email)**: 2FA after email link clicked. Completion: 81%.
- **Variant C (at first payment)**: 2FA only when funding card. Completion: 89%.

## Fraud Metrics

- Variant A false positives: 2.1% (stricter gating)
- Variant C false positives: 5.8% (but volume 2.3x higher)
- Chargeback rate stable across all variants: 0.34%

## Next Steps

- Run Variant B+C mixed cohort to find friction/safety tradeoff
- SMS delivery latency adds 8-15 seconds; testing email auth as fallback
- 18-25 age group shows 18% higher friction with any 2FA prompt
