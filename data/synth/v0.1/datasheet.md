# Datasheet: daydreamd synthetic corpus v0.1

Follows Gebru et al., "Datasheets for Datasets" (2021). Fields marked "TBD, filled at build time" are completed by the build script's output before the corpus is frozen; a datasheet with TBD fields means the corpus has not been frozen yet.

## Motivation

- **Purpose.** Ground truth by construction for the v0.1 micro-experiment: does a distance-forced pair sampler plus a critic recover cross-domain connections that were planted in a corpus, at a higher rate than random pairing, without firing on decoy pairs? See ADR-013.
- **Creator.** Julian Caraulani, solo, for the daydreamd project.
- **Funding.** None. Model calls ran on the author's own subscription.

## Composition

- **Instances.** Three kinds: 60 markdown notes (the corpus), 12 bridges (planted connections between two notes), 6 decoy pairs (note pairs that share vocabulary but no bridge).
- **Counts.** 60 notes. 12 planted pairs. 6 decoy pairs. Remaining note pairs (about 1,750) are unlabelled and assumed unconnected; that assumption is a known limitation.
- **Sample of a larger set?** No. The corpus is authored whole.
- **Fields per note.** `notes/<slug>.md`: title, body (150 to 400 words), YAML front matter with `topic` and `written_by` (model snapshot).
- **Fields per bridge** (`bridges.yaml`): `id`, `note_a`, `note_b`, `connection` (at most 40 words), `mechanism`, `testable_implication`, `domain_a`, `domain_b`.
- **Fields per decoy** (`decoys.yaml`): `id`, `note_a`, `note_b`, `shared_vocabulary`, `why_no_bridge`.
- **Labels.** `gold.json`: `{planted: [[note_a, note_b, bridge_id], ...], decoys: [[note_a, note_b, decoy_id], ...]}`.
- **Missing data.** None by design.
- **Relationships.** Bridges and decoys reference notes by slug.
- **Splits.** None. The whole corpus is the test set; there is no training.
- **Errors and noise.** The leakage check bounds how directly a note states its bridge: no six-word run (6-gram) of any gold connection or gold implication appears in any note. Build result 2026-09-13: 0 leakage failures, 0 regenerations for leakage. Style check: 0 em dashes. Length: 56 of 60 notes fall in the specified 150 to 300 words; 4 notes are between 119 and 149 words and were kept (logged as a deviation in PREREGISTRATION.md).
- **External resources.** None. Self-contained.
- **Confidential or personal data.** None. The builder is fictional. Any resemblance to a real person's projects is checked by the author before release.
- **Offensive content.** None.

## Collection process

- **Acquisition.** Bridges and decoys were written by hand. Notes were generated by a model from a prompt that lists, for each note, its topic and the half-bridges it must express implicitly. The prompt is committed at `prompts/synth_note_writer.md` with a version header.
- **Software.** `daydreamd synth build` (in `src/daydreamd/synth/`), pinned by `uv.lock`.
- **Note-writing model snapshot.** `claude-haiku-4-5-20251001` via headless `claude -p` (Claude Code 2.1.270), accessed 2026-09-13, provider default temperature, no seed control, prompt `prompts/synth_note.md` version 2. Measured list-price cost of the build: $1.54 (zero marginal cost on a subscription).
- **Who and compensation.** The author. No paid annotators.
- **Time frame.** Authored 2026-09. The fictional timeline inside the notes spans 2024 to 2026.
- **Ethical review.** None required: no human subjects, no personal data.
- **Consent.** N/A, fictional.

## Preprocessing

- **Steps.** Leakage check (bridge text against note chunks). Secret-pattern scan (should find nothing; recorded). Manifest with per-file SHA-256.
- **Raw data available?** The notes are the raw data. The generation prompt and the model's raw responses are committed under `data/synth/v0.1/build/`.
- **Software available?** Yes, in this repository.

## Uses

- **Used for.** daydreamd v0.1 preregistered experiment (`PREREGISTRATION.md`).
- **Other uses.** Any generate-and-select ideation pipeline that wants planted ground truth; testing concept-card extractors; testing duplicate-detection thresholds.
- **Caveats.** The notes are model-written, so a generator from the same model family may find the planted bridges easier than it would in human prose (shared imagination, arXiv:2407.16604). Report the two snapshot IDs together. Recovery on this corpus is not evidence of real-world novelty.
- **Uses to avoid.** Do not report a recovery rate here as "novel idea generation". Do not train on it and then evaluate on it.
