# Last Round Data

About 40% of applications rejected at the eligibility stage on the last call. The eligibility check examines company age (with a minimum threshold), headcount (with a minimum floor), the maturity of the tech claim, and two other signals still being isolated from rejection patterns.

## Writing and Waiting

Three to four weeks from submission before you get the eligibility decision. Just worked through that timeline on the most recent round. Standard wait time for these calls.

## Eligibility Signals

Confirmed so far: company age has a minimum. Headcount has a minimum. Your technology claim needs proper documentation backing it. Two more criteria that the rejection data is revealing, though I haven't pinned them down yet. Five total assessment points.

## Current Work

Compiling the full five-factor assessment across recent calls. The core criteria hold consistently, but each call seems to weight factors slightly differently. Worth tracking which rejections correlate with which criteria.
