## Fund Instrument 1  
- Opened on March 20th, 2023  
- Deadline May 15th, 2023  
- Up to €75k available per proposal  
- Focuses on digital innovation projects  

## Fund Instrument 2  
- Opened on January 1st, 2023  
- Extended deadline June 30th, 2023  
- Up to €100k available overall for consortiums  
- Emphasizes sustainable energy solutions  

## Key Differences  
- Instrument 1: Smaller individual grants, shorter application window  
- Instrument 2: Larger total funds, longer application period  
- Scope: Digital vs. Sustainable Energy
