## Recent Timesheet Submissions

The monthly timesheets for our two partners have been submitted late in seven of the last twelve months. The partner who submits quarterly reports has never missed a deadline. Each partner spends about an hour preparing their monthly timesheets, though this time seems to increase as deadlines approach.

## Upcoming Tasks

Reviewing and finalizing the application for the upcoming EU grant is ongoing. We aim to submit it by the end of next week. Ensuring all required documentation is up-to-date remains a priority.

## Communication with EU Bodies

We've had two successful video calls this month with the EU funding body, discussing the progress and addressing any concerns they may have. The next call is scheduled for mid-next month.

## Next Steps

Finalizing the budget section for the grant application is the primary focus now. We plan to complete it by early next week to ensure a smooth submission process.
