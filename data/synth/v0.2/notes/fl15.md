# Hydration and caffeine cutoff

## Daily hydration
- Target: 2.5L by 6pm, then maintenance only (0.5L evening max)
- Morning: 500ml with breakfast, 500ml by 10am
- Midday: 500ml at lunch, 500ml by 3pm
- Tracking: marked on workshop calendar

## Caffeine schedule
- Last intake: 2pm hard stop
- Morning coffee: 7:30am (one cup, approximately 95mg)
- Afternoon option: 11am green tea if needed (approximately 25mg)
- No evening herbal teas, check labels for hidden stimulants

## Observations
- Skipping lunch water hit drops focus by 4pm on client calls
- Missing hydration target correlates with next-day mood dips
- 2pm cutoff shows clean sleep onset, no late anxiety spikes

## Tracking method
- Filled bottle on desk, visual progress check
- Log cutoff time in calendar when last cup consumed
- Monthly pattern review ties to energy stability across store hours
