# Pitch deck feedback from Anna, 2026-09-10

## The review

Anna reviewed the current deck against EU grant scoring rubrics. Flagged three structural gaps:
- Revenue trajectory chart lacks YoY variance breakdown; grants want to see seasonality handled
- "Technology moat" section conflates two separate claims: proprietary dataset sourcing vs. fraud detection model performance
- Deployment cost per unit doesn't show unit economics at different scales (10k/100k/1M)

## Action items

- Recut revenue chart with quarterly data, flag payment processing peaks (Jan, Black Friday window)
- Split tech section: one subsection on data ingestion quality (competitor landscape), one on model drift handling under production load
- Add cost sensitivity table with assumption ranges (GPU pricing +/- 15%)

## Feedback signals

Anna noted that Horizon Europe evaluators score "repeatable, documented advantage" higher than "novel idea". Emphasis shifts from what you could build to what you've already demonstrated under constraints.

Flagged that deck currently reads as "we have technology" rather than "we have market". Suggested leading with customer LTV data, then deriving tech requirements from that constraint.
