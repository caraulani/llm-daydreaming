## Observations

Manual review holds closed in the last hour before the 24-hour SLA are reversed on appeal three times as often as earlier decisions. The queue backs up toward the SLA cutoff every afternoon, and reviewers clear whatever is left at 16:00.

## Actions Taken

Reviewed flagged transactions for irregularities. Noted increased appeals in the last hour before cutoffs. Adjusted strategies to manage end-of-day backlogs more effectively.

## Notes

Appeal reversals trend higher post-4 PM. Queues swell towards evening hours, impacting efficiency.
