## Delivery Scheduling Insights

Subscribers who select a delivery day that aligns with their initial shopping day tend to cancel less frequently. The default is Wednesday, but most users stick with the standard setting. A noticeable trend shows lower cancellation rates among those opting for matching days.

## Operational Adjustments

Adjusting delivery schedules to match customer preferences seems to reduce churn. Tests indicate a positive correlation between customized delivery options and retention rates. Monitoring these patterns helps in refining future strategies without explicit intervention.
