NCPs handle calls differently by member state

## Call rules variance

- Same Horizon Europe call, different eligibility reading across NCPs
- Polish NCP: consortium must include SME as lead, confirmed in writing
- Spanish NCP: no explicit SME lead requirement, flexible interpretation
- German NCP: added "German entity or German-based partner" language not in official call text
- French NCP: retroactive cost eligibility from month before submission, not standard

## Timeline issues

- Polish NCP: 30-day response window for clarifications (closed Sep 2025)
- Spain: rolling guidance, updates posted mid-month without notice
- Germany: telephone office hours Wed 14:00-16:00 CET only
- France: written queries take 15 business days average

## Practical friction

- No unified portal, emailing separate addresses
- Rules not consolidated anywhere, have to call each one
- Two NCPs contradicted each other on eligible costs, verified both
- One NCP's clarification deleted after 3 weeks, no archive

## Action

- Document each NCP's exact reading before submission
- File queries in writing to all four, keep timestamped copies
- Budget for country-specific compliance costs in proposal narrative
