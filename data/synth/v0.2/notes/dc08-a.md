## Incident Details
On 12/03/2023:
- Workshops main breaker tripped, cutting power to IoT sensors and gateway.
- Space heater in use during break time.

## Actions Taken
- Reconnected main breaker after confirming no electrical issues.
- Checked sensor data post-reset: readings returned to normal within 5 minutes.

## Next Steps
- Install timer on space heater to prevent overuse.
- Schedule routine maintenance of workshop wiring and breakers.
