Promo series refund spike

## Timing

Three promos back-to-back starting mid-March. Refund requests jumped reliably two days after each code dropped. Same pattern three times. Dip between campaigns, spike again.

## Order breakdown

Refunds clustered in small orders, most under 15 euros. Almost all had a card added at checkout rather than a saved method. Few repeat customers in the refund set.

## Support processing

Team clearing refund queue quickly. Approving without pulling payment details or order context into the decision. Fast throughput, light on verification.

## Notes

Two-day lag is tight and consistent across all three campaigns. The refund orders have a distinct profile: small value, fresh card entry. Worth looking at whether support should be pulling more signal before approving, especially if we run more campaigns soon. Pattern might not repeat but worth watching the next cycle.
