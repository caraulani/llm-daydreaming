name: Subscription Cancellation Report Dec 2023
description: Analysis of cancellations during competitor discount event
type: project

## Summary
- Significant increase in cancellations observed
- Noted on December 15th to 24th
- Competitor launched discount campaign on Dec 17th

## Key Facts
- Total subscriptions decreased by 8% within the analyzed period
- Average cancellation rate per day was 3%
- Most affected categories: Basic & Premium plans

## Actions Taken
- Reviewed payment processing logs for anomalies
- No fraud detected, likely due to competitor promotions
- Sent email reminders to existing customers on Dec 20th

## Next Steps
- Monitor customer feedback for potential reasons
- Plan promotional offers for January to retain subscribers
