## Current setup

Tracking daily compliance against my protocol. Morning checklist, evening log, weekly rollup. Using a simple spreadsheet with flags for adherence and deviations. Data exports to a local SQLite for trend analysis.

## Screening and intake

Added a short questionnaire for contraindication checks before any new supplement or intervention. Fills out intake form, then I review for flags. The screening happens after purchase, not before. Learned this the hard way: fair number of refunds when someone fails screening and we reverse the order. Better to catch it upfront, but workflow timing meant the questionnaire came downstream. Reworking the sequence.
