## Portal Submission Bounced

Submitted to Horizon Europe call (deadline 22 Sep), rejected same day for exceeding 2-page maximum. Abstract was 2.3 pages in their system though PDF editor showed 2.1. Character overflow by ~400 chars.

## What Happened

- Portal's page counter doesn't match standard rendering
- Didn't test upload render before final submission
- Cut methodology from 4 paragraphs to 3, still overflowed
- Support response said "trim to 2 pages exactly, no footnotes counted"

## Fix

- Remove background subsection (60 words saved)
- Compress objectives to bullet list instead of prose
- Move one citation to bibliography instead of inline
- Test again in portal before resubmit: paste into their textarea, not PDF
- Resubmit Friday (3-day buffer)

## Lesson for Next Call

Portal systems have invisible margins. Always build 10% buffer when no explicit character limit given, only page count. Easier to cut than rewrite under time pressure.
