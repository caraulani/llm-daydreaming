Thursday evening newsletter to Friday morning spike

## Send mechanics

Sending Thursday 8pm to subscriber base of 4,200. Click-through rate holds steady at 8-12%, mostly on new product features or restocks. Timing matters: too early and Friday shoppers miss the momentum, too late and people don't see it before bed.

## Friday morning order pattern

Orders spike 7am-11am, up 35-40% vs baseline weekday average. Revenue bump lasts until midday, then settles. Weekend orders trail behind this window significantly. Conversion is higher on Fridays following a send vs Fridays without one.

## Active variables

Subject line affects open rate more than send time. Product imagery in the email drives traffic better than copy length. Newsletter includes one discount code, rotating 10-15% off: code-specific orders are trackable and usually hit 60-70 units on high-traffic sends.

## Subscription dynamics

Open rate drifts down each Thursday if previous Friday didn't have a visible bump. Unsubscribes spike if sends happen more than once a week. List cleaning every 90 days recovers a few percentage points of engagement. Current churn sits at 3% monthly.
