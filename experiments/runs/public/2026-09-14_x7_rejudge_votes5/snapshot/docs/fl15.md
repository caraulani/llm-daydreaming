## Electrolyte timing on long ride days

## Current protocol
- Start with 500mg sodium in first bottle, mix with carbs and water. Sip at km 0-15.
- Second bottle (km 15-40) with lighter mix, 250mg sodium, focus on fluids over electrolytes.
- After 40km, switch to third bottle with 300mg sodium plus 150mg potassium (banana powder or electrolyte tabs).
- Aim for total 1200-1500mg sodium across 90+ minute rides. Track in spreadsheet with ride date, duration, temp.

## Variables to monitor
- Outside temp below 10C: reduce sodium slightly, body retains more.
- Rides over 90 minutes: cramping in legs indicates potassium gap in hour 2.
- Food intake matters: if eating food during ride (protein bar, etc), lower electrolyte concentration by 25%.
- Post-ride fluid: drink 150% of weight lost within 2 hours, with 40-60mg sodium per 100ml water.

## Notes from recent long days
- 2026-09-08, 140km at 18C: started with too much sodium in first bottle, nausea at km 35. Diluted second bottle more.
- 2026-09-11, 105km at 12C: potassium timing worked well, zero leg cramps. Repeat this pattern.
- Watch sweat rate: on high effort days, increases to 800ml/hr. Adjust sodium dose upward.
