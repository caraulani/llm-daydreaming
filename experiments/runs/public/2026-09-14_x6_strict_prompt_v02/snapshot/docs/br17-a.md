Billing cycle exit points

## Cancellation patterns this quarter

Charted exit points across the subscriber lifecycle. First billing cycle holds steady at 4 percent churn. Climbs to 6 percent on the second cycle. Third cycle is the sharp drop zone, 19 percent exit. Settles back to 5 percent for the fourth cycle and beyond. The numbers are small but the pattern is consistent across cohorts.

## Delivery cadence

Confirmed with logistics: nothing leaves the warehouse between scheduled deliveries. Subscribers receive product only on drop dates, no interim contact or shipping. Worth tracking whether this pattern correlates with the cycle-three spike.

## Return customer tracking

Pulled the repeat-purchase data. Cohort exiting at cycle three rarely reorders. Return rate for that group is near zero. Earlier cycles show higher return likelihood. Later cycles stabilize. This shapes the funnel model.
