# Protocol Run Notes

## Current Setup

Started cycle Sept 1. Using daily checklist in Apple Health plus manual log. Morning baseline at 7am, evening check at 9pm. Temperature, resting HR, mood on 1-10 scale. Measurements taking about 3 minutes total.

## Week One to Three Window

First two weeks clean. Week three hit the usual friction point around day 19, energy dip. That's the dropout window for most people I've seen tracking protocols. Had to anchor it harder: moved logging to coffee time (non-negotiable habit already), set phone reminder for evening. Worked.

## Effects Timeline

Nothing visible week one or two. Week four started showing resting HR trending down, sleep quality log correlating with days where I hit protocol targets. Small but measurable. Skipped Thursday and Friday middle of week three. Almost quit. Missing two days back-to-back is the actual risk point, not the week three slump.

## Staying In

Checkpoint at day 30. Morning routine locked in, evening one still floating. Temperature readings most consistent metric. HR data still too noisy. Mood scores useful for retroactive pattern matching. Visible shifts now make the friction feel worthwhile.
