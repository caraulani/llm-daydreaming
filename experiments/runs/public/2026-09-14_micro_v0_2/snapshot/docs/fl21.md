Soldering tip oxidation management

## Tip wear pattern
Chisel tip oxidises within 20-30 minutes of heavy use. Plating wears through, base copper exposed, oxidation accelerates. Conical tips hold up longer but more difficult to work with fine traces and tight pin spacing.

## Current setup
Hakko FX-888D with brass wool cleaner. Wet sponge makes oxidation worse, not better. Need flux before each joint or tip blacks out completely and won't wet solder. Cleaning routine: brass wool, flux, tin.

## What works
Keep flux pen on the bench within reach. Wipe on brass wool between joints. Tin the tip immediately after cleaning. Store in standby mode between sessions, never let it cool completely.
