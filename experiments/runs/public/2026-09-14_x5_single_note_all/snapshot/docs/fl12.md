## 28 Mar 2023 - App A Test
- Enabled two-factor authentication during signup
- Increase in initial signup drop-offs from 15% to 22%
- No change in fraud reports post-launch

## 14 Apr 2023 - Consumer Feedback
- Users complained about extra steps
- Some praised security, but few switched off TFA

## 29 May 2023 - App B Comparison
- Implemented streamlined TFA process
- Initial drop-off reduced to 18%
- Fraud detection improved by 15% in Q2

## Lessons Learned
- Simplified TFA increases signup rates
- Security improvements can reduce fraud without hurting user experience
