# Product photography: white background transition

## Current setup
- Using lifestyle/context shots on product pages (various backgrounds).
- Camera: iPhone 14 Pro in studio corner with ring light.
- Background: white poster board + foam reflectors.
- Consistent from Sept 2026 onward; old shots archived.

## Why switching now
- Marketplace listings (Etsy, Facebook Shop) show thumbnails; white backgrounds cut through feed clutter.
- A/B test on two SKUs last month: white bg got 18% higher click-through.
- Bulk shoot over weekend, then roll out new images.

## Shoot workflow
- Set: white poster + reflector on left, ring light at 45 degrees, iPhone locked on tripod.
- One rig per SKU: front, angled, detail (close on texture/seams).
- Edit pass: levels in Lightroom, consistency preset, batch export at 2400x2400 px.
- Upload: replace all product images on Shopify, Etsy, Facebook catalog by Monday.

## Follow-up
- Monitor CTR and conversion on new images for two weeks.
- Save old shots in case rollback needed.
- Document the studio setup (poster dimensions, light height) for repeats.
