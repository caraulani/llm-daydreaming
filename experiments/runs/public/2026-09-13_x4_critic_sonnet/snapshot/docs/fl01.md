# Packing Checklist Revamp After Mislabel

## The incident

Shipped order #5847 (customer in DE) with wrong item in box on Sept 7. Ordered burgundy lamp shade, received grey. Box label correct, contents wrong. Found during customer complaint Sept 8. Reship cost plus courier fees, roughly €28. Warehouse staging area crowded that day, multiple orders in flight simultaneously.

## Current checklist gaps

- No barcode scan at final stage before seal. Picking slip matched but visual verification skipped.
- Staff packing four orders at once. Boxes stacked, contents easy to mix.
- Label printed once from picking slip. If item swapped mid-pack, label never updated.

## New procedure

- Barcode scan of item versus picking slip before box seal. Required step.
- One order per station. Sequential staging, no parallels.
- Reprint label if any substitution. Cross-check with packing slip.
- Photo record of open boxes for custom orders. Adds time but catches errors early.

## Follow-up

Testing new flow with Sept 12 batch, 18 orders. Time impact roughly 90 seconds per order. Customer affected received full refund plus expedited replacement arriving Sept 15.
