# Soldering station setup: tip oxidation fix

## Current setup
- Hakko FX-888D, running 380°C for most work, 420°C for headers
- Solder: 63/37 tin/lead, leaded flux (Kester 44)
- Brass wool cleaner in use since March, switched from wet sponge
- Tips lasting 2-3 weeks before heavy oxidation layer builds

## Oxidation problem
- Black/brown crusty coating appears on tips after 5-7 days of moderate use
- Brass wool cleans surface but doesn't prevent regrowth
- Tinning after each joint helps briefly, oxidation returns within hours
- Temperature isn't the issue; even at 380°C tips oxidise

## Changes made in August
- Added wet sponge back alongside brass wool, using sponge first then brass
- Switching to lead-free solder flux (Kester 186) for comparison batch
- Keeping spare tips (Hakko T18-D24 and T18-D16) rotated in
- Storing used tips in sealed container with flux-soaked sponge

## Next check
- Tracking tip life with lead-free flux vs leaded, logging dates
- If oxidation still appears in next 10 days, trying tinning compound (Thinol or Thermalloy)
- Temperature test: may push to 410°C consistently if flux isn't the limiting factor
