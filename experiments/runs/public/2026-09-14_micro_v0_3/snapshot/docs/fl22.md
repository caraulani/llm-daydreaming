LoRa Antenna Roof Test

## Setup
- Placed 3 antennas on roof corners, April 10th
- Connected to router in workshop via cable

## Equipment
- Model: Semtech SX1276, range up to 5km open field
- Power supply: 3.3V DC stable output from bench PSU

## Results
- RSSI test showed -90dBm at optimal position
- Far corner had weaker signal, -110dBm
- Middle antenna provided most consistent readings
