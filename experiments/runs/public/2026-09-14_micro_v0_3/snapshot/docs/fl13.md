## Progression targets

Q3 goal: +5kg bench, +10kg squat, +8kg deadlift. Periodize with 4-week accumulation, deload week 4. Secondary lifts: 3 sets each, upper/lower split. Track RPE and bar speed on video weekly.

## Mesocycle layout

Weeks 1-3: rep range 6-8, increase load each session. Week 4: deload at 60% intensity. Weeks 5-7: rep range 5-6, higher intensity. Week 8: test weeks. Conditioning: 15 minutes post-workout, not competing with strength.
