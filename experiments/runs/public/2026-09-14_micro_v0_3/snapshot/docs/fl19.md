# Impact Section Feedback: 2024 Round

## What Reviewer Flagged

- Claimed 150 SME users by month 6, but no baseline market size context. Reviewer asked: compared to what?
- "Cost per user" metric too narrow, doesn't surface the real value to payment platforms or gig workers
- Timeline: prototype to scale-up in 3 months felt compressed. No phasing story
- Adoption targets stated but not tied to any pilot data or comparable deployments

## Missing Pieces

- No segment breakdown. Impact on e-commerce store owners vs. fraud detection teams vs. platform operators differs sharply
- Zero comparison to what existing systems achieve in same space
- LLM evaluation claims read as aspiration, not pilot-backed

## Approach for v2

- Lead with addressable market size: EU SMEs, calling platforms, grant-maker regions first
- Show 2024 pilot numbers upfront before any projection
- Add risk section: what blocks adoption, how we'd unblock it
- Split impact claims cleanly: direct (people using product) vs. indirect (platform effects, fraud reduction)
