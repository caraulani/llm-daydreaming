Carrier contract flipped 2 August. New terms: Tuesday dispatch cutoff 06:30, everything else stays 11:00. This ripples into packing timing.

## Tuesday morning pack

Window is 05:45 to 06:30. Non-stop, no break in there. Orders have to be boxed, weighed, labeled, loaded. Running this tight means Monday night prep is critical, customer orders locked down before the pack sprint starts.

## Other weekdays

Monday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday all keep 11:00 cutoff. Breathing room. Overnight orders soak, sort, stage normally. Most volume doesn't hit Monday evening anyway.

## Early days on the new rhythm

Rates improved enough to justify the squeeze. Running against the clock Tuesday mornings is doable if the system doesn't slip. Had one close call already. Watching whether any order types should get deferred to the 11:00 days if they're edge cases.
